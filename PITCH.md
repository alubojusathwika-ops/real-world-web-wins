# Ember & Olive — Website Pitch

## The problem

Most independent restaurants live and die by walk-ins and word of mouth. But
today, "word of mouth" happens on Google, Instagram, and TripAdvisor before it
ever happens on the street. When someone searches for **"restaurant near me"**
or your name, what they see in the first three seconds decides whether they
walk in — or walk past to the place next door.

Right now, many local restaurants either have **no website** or an outdated
one that looks worse than the food. That gap costs real covers every week.

## What this website does

This site is designed to convert curious searchers into booked tables.

- **A strong first impression.** A cinematic hero, warm palette, and editorial
  typography that matches the quality of the food. Guests judge the restaurant
  in seconds — the site earns their trust immediately.
- **A menu they can actually read.** Clearly categorised, priced, and tagged
  (signature, vegetarian, new). No PDF downloads, no pinch-to-zoom.
- **Frictionless reservations.** A one-screen booking form on `/reserve`.
  Every page CTA leads here.
- **Social proof.** Testimonials on the home page. A gallery of the room and
  the plates for Instagram cross-posting.
- **Google-ready.** Every page has its own unique title, description, and
  social preview. The `/visit` page includes structured data (LocalBusiness
  schema) so Google can show hours, address, and cuisine directly in search
  results.
- **Mobile-first.** 70%+ of restaurant searches happen on a phone. The site
  works beautifully from 360px up.

## Growth levers built in

1. **SEO for local queries** — route-per-page structure means Google can index
   "menu", "book a table", "opening hours" as separate pages, boosting your
   presence for high-intent searches.
2. **Reservation capture** — every visitor is nudged toward `/reserve`.
3. **Google Business Profile tie-in** — the address, hours, and phone match
   what's on Google Maps, which strengthens local ranking.
4. **Instagram-friendly gallery** — a professional-quality gallery you can
   point to from bio links.

## What's next (upgrade path)

The demo intentionally keeps things simple. When the restaurant is ready:

- **Custom domain** (e.g. `emberandolive.co`) — connectable in one click.
- **Real reservation backend** — bookings sent to email + saved to a database,
  with SMS/email confirmations.
- **CMS for menu edits** — the chef updates the menu in a simple dashboard,
  no developer needed.
- **Online ordering & payments** — takeaway and gift vouchers.
- **Email list** — capture emails for tasting menus and seasonal openings.
